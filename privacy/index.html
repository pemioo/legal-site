Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Your local aquarium archive

Pemio stores aquarium profiles, water readings, species records, health observations, maintenance records, photos, audio voice notes and generated reports on your device. Pemio does not require an account and does not operate a server for your aquarium content.

Camera, photos and microphone

With your permission, Pemio uses the camera to capture aquarium photos, reads the photos you select and uses the microphone to record voice notes. You can decline these permissions and continue using text records. You can manage access in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Pemio asks for notification permission when you first visit the aquarium overview. Scheduled care reminders are generated on your device. The app does not use notifications for advertising.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. Pemio stores verified transaction identifiers and the remaining report balance on your device to deliver purchased reports and prevent duplicate delivery. Pemio does not receive your payment card information.

Exports and backups

CSV files, PDF reports and aquarium backups are shared only when you choose a destination in the system share interface. If you choose a third-party destination, that provider receives the files you select and its privacy terms apply. You decide whether to agree to that sharing. iOS device backup and Files storage are controlled by your Apple settings.

Network access

Pemio uses Apple services for purchases and loads its legal pages over HTTPS. The legal page hosting provider may process ordinary connection information such as IP address and browser request details. Aquarium photos, audio and records are not sent to that provider.

Data protection and deletion

Local archive files use iOS file protection. You can delete individual records or erase aquarium data from Your Local Archive. Erasing aquarium data preserves the current purchase balance and transaction history. Uninstalling the app can remove local data, including the remaining balance. Export records you wish to retain.

Children and sensitive information

Pemio is an aquarium record-keeping tool. Do not place sensitive personal information in your aquarium notes. Pemio does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when data practices change. Contact: pemio.aquarium.care.placeholder@gmail.com.
